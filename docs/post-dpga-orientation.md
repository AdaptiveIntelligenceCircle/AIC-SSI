# Post-DPGA Orientation

After AIC's engagement with Digital Public Goods and
public-interest infrastructure discussions,
AIC-SSI is interpreted primarily as a governance
and human-agency repository.

Identity is treated as:

- a trust problem,
- an agency problem,
- a governance problem,

not merely an authentication problem.

Future work should remain aligned with:

- Human Agency
- Non-Capture
- Public Interest
- Transparency
- Institutional Resilience

No claim is made regarding deployment,
certification, compliance, or adoption.
