**Last updated:** November 2026  
**Status:** Early exploration  
**Guiding constraint:** Remain secondary to Parallel TestNet and core ethical kernel progress. Prefer design clarity over implementation speed.

---

## Current Phase (late 2026 – 2027)

**Focus:** Conceptual clarity and alignment with AIC principles

- Clarify the relationship between SSI ideas and Human Meaning / human agency
- Document key risks (capture, over-reliance, loss of human responsibility)
- Keep design notes consistent with under-claim and Non-Capture posture
- Avoid presenting experimental code as ready or authoritative

---

## Near-term (capacity-dependent)

Possible work items (not commitments):

- Further design notes on recovery, agency, and fail-closed behavior
- Limited illustrative examples that demonstrate constraints rather than features
- Alignment checks with updates coming from AIC-Legal, AIC-DPG-Alignment, and TestNet learning

---

## Explicit non-goals for the current period

- Building a production identity system
- Claiming quantum-resistance or long-term cryptographic guarantees as achieved
- Positioning AIC-SSI as a drop-in SSI solution for external organizations
- Expanding scope in ways that increase capture risk or dilute under-claim

---

## Longer horizon (post-TestNet maturation)

Any more ambitious work will only be considered after:

- Parallel TestNet Phase 0 has clearer, stable artifacts
- Core ethical evaluation patterns are better understood in practice
- The project can maintain non-capture and human-agency constraints with higher confidence

Until then, restraint is the correct posture.

---

*Progress in AIC-SSI is measured by the quality of constraints and clarity of thinking, not by the number of features announced.*
